How TrustCheck Works

A transparent explanation of the multi-layer verification system, phishing link detection, and practical student safety rules.

The Multi-Layer Verification Engine

TrustCheck evaluates queries deterministically across complementary defensive layers:

  • Layer 1 — Starter Verified Database: Evaluates target names against authenticated enterprises (Google, Microsoft, TCS, Infosys, Coursera, NPTEL, etc.). Distinguishes exact matches from partial matches.
  • Layer 2 — Scam Keyword Heuristics: Inspects communication text for advance-fee requests, "guaranteed placement without interview", refundable deposits, and task scams.
  • Layer 3 — Link & Phishing Analysis: Analyzes application URLs against the company's authenticated domain, checks for suspicious TLDs (.xyz, .top), and flags generic Google Forms or Telegram channels used for hiring.
  • Layer 4 — Community Feedback: Crowdsourced database where students can search ongoing scam reports and flag new fraudulent contacts.

Essential Student Security Rules

Protect your career and finances with these baseline principles:

  • Never Pay for an Internship: Legitimate companies pay candidates. Any request for a "registration fee", "laptop security deposit", or "processing charge" is a scam.
  • Inspect Corporate Email & Website Domains: Verify that recruiter communications originate from @company.com, not free Gmail, Outlook, or suspicious lookalike domains (e.g. tcs-jobs.xyz).
  • Dataset Limitations: The starter repository covers 18 verified entities. An UNVERIFIED status means the entity is not in the local starter database; students should conduct manual due diligence.
